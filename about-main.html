Rhode Island Red × Fayoumi cross. Selected for BD climate resilience, dual-purpose output (meat + egg), lower feed conversion ratio, heat tolerance. Outperforms broilers on rooftops due to foraging temperament and disease resistance to local pathogens.

FERMENTED FEED — WINNER PROTOCOL

Kitchen waste + vegetable peelings + rice + trace yeast/molasses → sealed airtight bucket → 2–3 days anaerobic fermentation. Pathogenic bacteria eliminated. Nutrient density ~3× increased. Feed self-preserved. Single-daily-visit constraint makes this the ONLY viable wet feed method.

AnaerobicYeast + MolassesZero-Waste3× Nutrients
⚠️
WET FEED RISK — ELIMINATED

Open wet feed becomes dangerous within hours: bacterial + fungal invasion, fly egg-laying (maggot infestation), putrefaction causing duckling diarrhea. Open wet food is categorically prohibited. Only fermented or dry feed during single daily rounds.

Fungal RiskSingle Visit ProtocolMandatory Rule
🐦
QUAIL DIET PROTOCOL

Quail bills too small for whole seeds. Protocol: broken wheat (গমের খুদ) or rice grits mixed with BSF larvae. Fermented grain base + fresh larvae = complete protein + carbohydrate. No whole seeds. BSF larvae fed whole — quail handle them without preparation.

Broken WheatBSF MixQuail-Specific

PIGEON — GRANIVORE ONLY

Pigeons are strict granivores. They naturally avoid insects and larvae. Feeding BSF larvae to pigeons is counterproductive. Feed: whole grain only — wheat, sorghum, millet, paddy. Zero larvae. Zero wet feed.

BSF LARVAE SACK FARMING — FULL ENGINEERING SPEC

STEP 01
SACK SETUP

10–20kg jute sack (চটের বস্তা). Position UPRIGHT (খাড়া) — never sideways. Pre-pupa instinctively crawl upward in darkness. Horizontal placement destroys the harvest mechanic entirely.

STEP 02
SUBSTRATE LOADING

Any kitchen waste including meat and citrus (unlike earthworms which reject these). Fill to 60–70% capacity. Do not pack tight — airflow channels needed. Moisture: damp, no dripping (squeeze test).

STEP 03
THE RAMP

Jute fabric strip or rough cardboard at 35–45° angle from substrate, up through 1.5–2cm exit hole at sack top. Smooth plastic CANNOT be used — larvae cannot grip it. Rough jute surface is required.

STEP 04
COLLECTION BOTTLE

1.5–2L plastic bottle mouth at ramp exit. Wrapped fully in black cloth — larvae are photophobic and race toward the dark space. Entry hole 1.5–2cm prevents birds from accessing with beak.

STEP 05
LEACHATE HANDLING

Raw BSF leachate: highly acidic + ammonia-rich. Direct use kills earthworms and damages roots. MANDATORY: dilute 1:10 ratio (1 part leachate + 10 parts water). Diluted = premium liquid fertilizer.

Apply heavy grease or recycled motor oil on all four stand legs. Rats cannot grip the slick surface — climbing is physically impossible. Near-zero cost (recycled oil). No chemicals near food area.

WATER MOAT SYSTEM

Stand legs inside small water-filled containers. Rats avoid crossing water obstacles. Passive humidity moderator as side benefit. Maintenance: weekly refill only.

BSF bottles and larvae bins surrounded by water bowls. Birds cannot land on water surface. No nets or wires required. Fully passive — no daily interaction needed.

FIPRONIL GEL BAIT (Cockroach)

Fipronil Gel applied via syringe in micro-dots at cage corners. Cockroaches consume → return to colony → die → colony mates eat corpse → cascade colony kill. Not individual kill — colony elimination.
