BSF SACK HARVESTING WORKFLOW

STEP 01
Upright Sack Setup

10–20kg jute sack positioned strictly upright. Larvae instinctively migrate upward toward dark collection points when ready to pupate.

STEP 02
Substrate Feeding

Kitchen waste, fruit peels, and scraps loaded at 60–70% capacity with adequate air channels for optimal larval digestion.

STEP 03
Rough Jute Ramp

Jute fabric strip positioned at a 35–45° incline leading to the exit portal. Provides necessary tactile grip for climbing pre-pupae.

STEP 04
Dark Collection Chamber

Black cloth-wrapped bottle catches photophobic larvae automatically without requiring manual sifting.
